Types of Airport Parking Explained by APH

APH Airport Parking facilitates various types of services, each with distinct advantages:

Park and Ride:

Typically a more budget-friendly choice. You park your car in a secure compound away from the airport. A complimentary shuttle bus then transfers you to the terminal.

Meet and Greet:

A highly convenient option where you drop off your car at the terminal. A chauffeur then parks it for you. Your car is returned to you upon your arrival. This is ideal for those with heavy luggage or tight schedules.

On-Site Parking:

You park your car directly at the airport's official car parks. These are usually the closest to the terminals. Consequently, this offers maximum convenience, though often at a higher price.
