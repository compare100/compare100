What you can compare

Compare Your Travel Insurance covers popular policy types. That makes it useful for many travellers.

  • Single trip cover for one holiday.
  • Annual multi-trip cover if you travel often.
  • Winter sports cover for skiing and snowboarding trips.
  • Cruise cover with cruise-specific protection.

How Compare Your Travel Insurance works

The process is straightforward. It follows three simple steps.

1) Tell them about your trip

Fill in a short form with your travel dates, destination, and any cover needs. You can choose single trip or annual cover. You can also add options like winter sports or cruise cover if required.

2) Compare quotes

You can compare prices and cover levels from different providers. You can also use filters to narrow down results. This helps you find a policy that fits your trip and budget.

3) Choose a policy

Once you decide, you’re sent to the provider to buy securely online. After purchase, policy documents are sent to your email. That keeps everything easy to find later.

Why use a comparison site for travel insurance?

Travel insurance can vary a lot. Prices change. Cover details change too. A comparison site helps you review options in one place. It can also save time when you need cover quickly.

Quick tips before you buy

  • Check medical cover and declare pre-existing conditions.
  • Match the policy to your trip, like cruise or winter sports.
  • Look at excess levels so you know what you may pay in a claim.
  • Confirm cancellation cover if you’ve pre-paid for your trip.
  • Choose annual cover if you plan several trips this year.
