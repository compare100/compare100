Important Considerations Before Joining DragonPass

While DragonPass Airport Lounge offers incredible benefits, it's vital to consider these points:

Membership Tier Costs:

Carefully evaluate which membership tier (annual vs. pay-per-visit) best suits your travel frequency. Therefore, assess the cost-effectiveness based on your individual needs.

Lounge Specifics:

Not all lounges offer every amenity (e.g., showers, spa services). Consequently, always check the specific lounge details via the app before your visit.

Lounge Capacity:

Some lounges might have limited capacity, especially during peak hours. Hence, arrival with sufficient time before your flight is always recommended.
