First Direct 1st Account

Last updated 2025-07-28 · Current Accounts · Compare100 editorial team

Affiliate disclosure: we may earn a commission if you take out a product through links on this page. It costs you nothing extra and does not influence how providers are listed.

First Direct 1st Account provides a compelling £175 welcome bonus for new UK customers in 2025. Beyond this attractive cash incentive, the First Direct 1st Account offers a valuable £250 interest-free overdraft, subject to status. Account holders, moreover, gain access to a highly competitive 7.00% AER/gross Regular Saver account, enhancing their savings potential. Furthermore, you benefit from no debit card fees when spending abroad, making it ideal for international use.

To qualify for the bonus, applicants must meet specific criteria within 45 days of opening the account. First, complete a full switch using the Current Account Switch Service (CASS). This must include at least two Direct Debits or standing orders. Secondly, deposit a minimum of £1,000. Thirdly, make five or more debit card payments. Lastly, log on to digital banking. This offer is exclusively for new customers who have not previously held a First Direct product. Additionally, it is not available to individuals who have opened an HSBC current account on or after January 1, 2018, as First Direct operates as part of the HSBC group. Notably, the First Direct 1st Account does not impose a minimum monthly pay-in requirement, offering greater flexibility.

The bonus is limited to one payment per customer or joint account. The funds are typically paid by the 20th of the month following the month in which all eligibility criteria have been successfully met. It is important to note that First Direct reserves the right to withdraw this offer at any time. The First Direct 1st Account itself carries no monthly fees, ensuring cost-effective banking.

First Direct consistently receives high ratings for its customer service. Key strengths frequently highlighted by customers include its 24/7 UK-based call centres, remarkably quick phone answer times (often within minutes), and the helpfulness and politeness of its staff, leading to efficient problem resolution. Smart Money People reviews show overwhelmingly positive sentiment. While the mobile app is sometimes described as basic, its functionality is deemed sufficient, with the primary strength of First Direct's service residing in its human interaction. First Direct's strategy appears to prioritize exceptional customer service, even when its cash bonus may not be the highest on the market. The consistent positive feedback, particularly concerning human interaction and swift problem resolution, suggests that for a significant segment of the market, reliable and accessible support is a more powerful long-term draw than a marginally larger cash bonus. This indicates that sustained customer satisfaction, particularly in addressing issues, is crucial for retention and positive word-of-mouth, which can ultimately be more valuable than aggressive marketing campaigns focused solely on monetary incentives.

Why Choose the First Direct 1st Account for Your Banking?

Choosing the First Direct 1st Account offers an appealing blend of financial incentives, competitive savings options, and unparalleled customer service. The attractive welcome bonus, coupled with an interest-free overdraft, provides immediate financial benefits. Moreover, access to a high-interest Regular Saver account and no foreign debit card fees directly enhance your long-term savings and international spending. Their commitment to 24/7 UK-based support and efficient problem resolution ensures a hassle-free and dependable banking experience. This ultimately makes the First Direct 1st Account an excellent option for individuals seeking comprehensive value and reliable financial services.
