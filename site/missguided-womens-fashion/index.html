Missguided Review: Prices, Returns and a Site That Moved

Rates and terms checked 20 November 2026 · Women's Fashion · Compare100 editorial team

Affiliate disclosure: we may earn a commission if you take out a product through links on this page. It costs you nothing extra and does not influence how providers are listed.

Missguided's cheapest dress on 20 November 2026 was $9.65, cut from $10.73, inside a womenswear range running to 5,785 items across 73 pages of listings. A site-wide code, SAVE15, took a further 15% off any four pieces, and the shop gives 30 days from delivery to change your mind. For a label built on £20 party dresses, the prices have held.

The address has not. Typing missguided.co.uk returns an HTTP 302 redirect to missguided.com/market-us, a United States storefront that declares itself en-US, prices everything in dollars and offers no way to switch to a UK or GB market on any page we could read. There is no pound price anywhere on it. That is not a criticism of the clothes, but it decides what you are shopping in before you add anything to a basket.

The brand still puts out new work: a Jayda Cheaves collaboration with Sumwon, the label founded by Missguided's own founder Nitin Passi, sat on the dresses page at $20.14. Alongside it the same shop now carries Playboy, Baby Phat, Everlast and a teen line, MG Teen. What follows is what the documents behind all of it say.

What $9.65 buys, and the discount that stacks on top

The dresses listing is the honest test of a fast-fashion shop, and Missguided's reads cheap without reading empty. Six prices taken straight off it on 20 November 2026: the Gothic Cross Print Cowl Neck Bodycon Mini Dress at $9.65 from $10.73; the Satin Mini Dress With Lace Sleeves And Asymmetric Hem at $22.02 from $24.47; the Lace Mini Dress With Corset Bodice And Long Sleeves at $30.43 from $38.04; the Lace Bodycon Mini Dress With Corset Detail Party at $33.87 from $37.64; the Satin Jacquard Mini Dress With Frog Button Detail at $37.71 from $41.90; and the Jayda Cheaves x Sumwon Off-Shoulder Bodycon Mini Dress at $20.14.

The percentage flags on those listings are real rather than decorative, which is worth saying because it often is not. Each discounted item carried a tag of -10% or -20%, and the arithmetic ties up to within a penny every time: 9.65 against 10.73 is 10.1% off, 30.43 against 38.04 is exactly 20%, and 37.71 against 41.90 is exactly 10%. The shop is not inflating a “was” price to manufacture a saving on these rows.

Stacked on top is a banner reading “Code: SAVE15” and “BUY 4, GET 15% OFF”. On four items at the $20 mark that is around $12 off an $80 basket, and it is a volume mechanic rather than a markdown: it rewards the fourth dress, not the first. The range itself is genuinely deep at 5,785 dress listings over 73 pages, which is more choice than most of the shops this one is compared with hold in the category.

The .co.uk address is not a shop any more

Both UK addresses answer the same way. www.missguided.co.uk returns HTTP 302 and so does the bare missguided.co.uk, and both point at https://www.missguided.com/market-us. The destination is not a holding page or a parked domain — it is a working shop, and on the evidence of the prices above a well-stocked one. It is simply an American shop. Its og:locale is declared as en-US, every canonical URL read carried the /market-us prefix, and the shipping page's country selector showed “United States” and nothing else.

We looked specifically for a route back. There is no market or country selector on the homepage, the brand landing pages, the contact page or the shipping page; no /market-gb or /market-uk path appears in any page source read; and the site's own category sitemap, which lists category addresses in the form /category/Women-Dresses-12716, contains no UK or GB variant across roughly 300 entries checked. The robots file disallows almost nothing — only /api/ for general crawlers — so the absence is not a crawling artefact. The pages that exist are the pages we read.

Whether a UK delivery address is accepted at checkout is the one thing this cannot settle, because establishing it would mean placing an order. No shipping table, delivery estimate or duty statement naming the United Kingdom was published anywhere we could reach. If you intend to order from Britain, that is the question to put to customer services before you pay, and it needs confirming rather than assuming.

Two live shipping pages, two different prices

Missguided publishes its shipping policy at two addresses, and on 20 November 2026 they did not agree. /page/shipping, which carries its own canonical tag and is one of only four addresses in the site's article sitemap, gave free standard delivery on “Orders over $70.00”, $5.99 below that, and $12.90 for express. /market-us/page/shipping, canonical to itself, gave free standard delivery on “Orders over $56.99”, $4.99 below that, and $9.99 for express. Same shop, same currency, same day, three figures different on each line.

We are therefore not printing a delivery price for this shop, because we cannot tell you which of its own two answers is the live one. The pattern is worth understanding rather than just noting: the four addresses listed in the site's article sitemap — shipping, returns, privacy and terms, all stamped with a 20 November 2026 modification date — are the /page/ versions, while the /market-us/ versions are the ones a shopper browsing the storefront is sent to. The copy a search engine is pointed at is not the copy the shop serves.

Both versions are also stale in a way that is easy to check. On 20 November 2026 one promised standard delivery “between Sep 3 - Sep 10” and express “between Sep 2 - Sep 9”; the other promised “between Oct 10 - Oct 17” and “between Oct 9 - Oct 16”. Neither window prints a year, and both had already passed. One page adds that “Shipping Time does not include a 1-3 day processing period”, which the other omits.

Thirty days on one page, damaged goods only on another

The returns page is clear and reasonably generous. “You have 30 days to decide if an item is right for you”, measured from delivery, with the request to be raised inside that window; “Our warehouse will automatically process your refund within 3 working days after your return parcel is received”; and a stated 5 business days to process once received on the other version of the page. Thirty days beats the 14-day statutory minimum a UK shopper would get on a distance sale, and the shop chose to give it.

The terms of use say something else. Clause 5.6 reads “Only damaged or defective items can be returned” — a change-of-mind return is exactly what that sentence excludes, and it sits in the document the terms themselves make the contract. Two of the merchant's own documents, both live, both linked from the same footer, give a buyer two different answers about the most important thing after price. The generous one is the one the shop will probably honour; it is not the one it has agreed to.

Three further details matter more than they look. Returns are “made at your own cost”, and the shop runs no collection service: “We do not offer Freight To Collect (FTC) service for packages returned to us.” The return shipping fee is said to vary by delivery country, but the table of amounts is published only as an image file hosted on a content delivery domain, so the figures are not in the page text and cannot be quoted here at all. And the non-returnable list is long for a shop selling party clothes: “bodysuits, lingerie, underwear, swimwear, jewelry, accessories, clearance items, and any other final sale items”. A reduced dress may be a final sale item, and nothing on the page says which.

Singapore law, one arbitrator, and the £5,000 line

Clause 1.1 of the terms, effective June 25, 2025, is the most useful paragraph on the site. “Depending on your market region, the Selling Entity is one of the following:” and then four companies — SHEIN Distribution Corporation for the United States, INFINITE STYLES ECOMMERCE CO., LIMITED for European Union Member States, SHEIN Distribution Canada Limited for Canada, and FASHION CHOICE PTE. LTD. for “all other markets”. The United Kingdom, the UK and Great Britain appear nowhere in the document. Since Britain is not the United States, not an EU member state and not Canada, a British buyer falls into the fourth bucket by elimination rather than by being addressed.

What governs that contract is set out plainly. The terms “shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Singapore”, with any dispute “administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre”. “The seat of the arbitration shall be Singapore.” “The Tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator.” “The language of the arbitration shall be English.” Clause 12.2 adds that “All claims and disputes must be arbitrated or litigated on an individual basis”, which rules out joining a group claim.

A UK reader should know how little of that survives contact with British law. The Competition and Markets Authority's own unfair contract terms guidance, CMA37, last updated 22 July 2026, records at footnote 107 that section 91 of the Arbitration Act 1996 “renders automatically unfair certain terms” “which bind a consumer to go to arbitration, where the sum of money in dispute does not exceed £5,000”. A dress is well inside that. The same guidance notes at paragraph 2.16 that Part 2 of the Consumer Rights Act “applies even if the contract states that the parties agree that it is to be” “governed by the law of another country”, where the contract has a close connection to the UK. The terms do acknowledge this, after a fashion: “Nothing in this Clause shall affect the statutory rights you as a consumer have”.

Five companies have carried the name; four are gone

The Companies House register returns six results for “Missguided”, five of them the retailer's own corporate family. The original trading company, number 03235904, was renamed MGL REALISATIONS (2022) LIMITED and is dissolved. Its filing history is the whole story in ten entries: appointment of an administrator filed 7 June 2022, a 47-page statement of administrator's proposals on 29 July 2022, notice of deemed approval on 11 August 2022, a 24-page statement of affairs on 3 October 2022, progress reports on 4 January 2023, 6 July 2023 and 2 January 2024, an extension of the administration on 19 May 2023, notice of the move from administration to dissolution on 2 June 2024, and the final Gazette notice on 2 September 2024. The registered office moved to care of Teneo Financial Advisory Limited in Birmingham when the administrators went in.

The company that held the brand itself, MISSGUIDED (IP) LIMITED, number 13797040, was incorporated on 13 December 2021 and renamed MGIP REALISATIONS (2022) LIMITED on 18 July 2022. Its status today is “Liquidation”, four years on. A receiver was appointed on 17 June 2022, an extraordinary resolution to wind up was passed on 22 November 2022, a voluntary liquidator was appointed on 5 January 2023, and liquidators' statements of receipts and payments have been filed to 21 November 2023, 2024 and 2025. On 12 August 2026 the liquidator was removed by court order and a replacement appointed the same day. Its accounts have been overdue since 13 September 2023 and its confirmation statement since 26 December 2022. Two more entities, MISSGUIDED GLOBAL LIMITED and an earlier MISSGUIDED RETAIL LIMITED, were both dissolved on 4 October 2022.

One company is still active, and it is the quietest fact here. MISSGUIDED RETAIL LIMITED, number 12298767, was incorporated on 5 November 2019 as SDI (RETAIL CO 13) LIMITED and took the Missguided name on 30 January 2023. Its registered office is Unit A, Brook Park East, Shirebrook NG20 8RY, and its stated nature of business is “74990 - Non-trading company”. Its accounts are up to date, made up to 27 April 2025 with the next due by 29 January 2027. Nitin Passi, who founded Missguided in 2009, appears on its register of officers as a director appointed on 22 June 2022 and resigned on 20 July 2022 — four weeks.

What the seller's own annual report called the brand

Frasers Group bought the Missguided business out of administration in 2022 and sold the intellectual property on. Its own Annual Report and Accounts for the 52 weeks ended 28 April 2024, whose Chair's statement is signed 17 July 2024, mentions the brand twice. The segment overview refers to “the Missguided website until the disposal of the Missguided intellectual property in October 2023”. The strategy pages list, among that year's achievements, “Disposed of non-core brand Missguided, enabling us to rationalise the number of brands within the Premium Lifestyle segment”.

Neither sentence names a buyer or a price, and no consideration figure for the disposal appears in the part of that report we were able to read. What does name a company is the shop's own contract, and it names Shein: clause 1.1 above makes SHEIN Distribution Corporation the selling entity in the United States and SHEIN Distribution Canada Limited the selling entity in Canada, for goods sold under the Missguided name.

Who holds the UK trade mark today we could not establish. The Intellectual Property Office's own register would not open on three separate attempts on 20 November 2026, and we will not take a proprietor's name from a commercial database and present it as the register. That one needs confirming, and it matters more than it sounds: the register is the only place a shopper can check who is entitled to trade under a name in Britain.

The regulators' record: five ASA rulings and one recall

The Advertising Standards Authority investigated Missguided Ltd five times between 2014 and 2021, and the record is more mixed than the brand's reputation suggests. Two investigations produced findings against it. On 4 March 2020 three complaints about two posters were partly upheld: one ad was cleared, while of the other the ASA concluded that “the sexually suggestive styling and pose would be seen as presenting women as sexual objects” under CAP Code rule 4.1, and ruled that it “must not appear again in its current form”. On 14 April 2021 an Instagram post on Zara McDermott's account promoting a Missguided edit was upheld on three complaints under rule 2.1, because it carried no advertising label: “The ad must not appear again in the form complained about.”

The other three went the brand's way, which is worth saying for balance. A television ad was cleared of implying immoderate drinking on 8 January 2020 after eight complaints. Two Playboy collaboration posters were cleared on 21 October 2020 after 18 complaints that the model looked under 18, the ASA finding she was 28 at the shoot. Four television broadcasts were cleared on 9 April 2014 after three complaints, the ASA concluding “that the ads were suitable to be broadcast at anytime”.

On the UK product safety register the keyword “missguided” returns 2 reports, of which one is the brand's own: “Product Recall: Missguided Right Now Brow Thickening Brow Gel”, case 2201-0020, recall dated 7 January 2022 and published 27 March 2022. The product was made in Poland in four shades and six batches, presented “a microbiological risk due to contamination” under the Cosmetic Products Enforcement Regulations 2013, was notified by the Office for Product Safety and Standards, and “has been recalled from end users”. For context, the keyword “womens clothing” returns 274 reports on the same register and the keyword “shein” returns 17, the most recent dated 30 September 2026.

Where it wins

  • Dresses genuinely start under $10 — $9.65 for a cowl-neck bodycon mini on 20 November 2026 — and the range is deep at 5,785 dress listings over 73 pages
  • The percentage discount flags are honest: every -10% and -20% tag checked tied up to within a penny against the higher price shown beside it
  • A stacking code, SAVE15, takes a further 15% off any four items, which is a real saving on a multi-item basket rather than a single-line markdown
  • The returns page gives 30 days from delivery to change your mind, more than twice the 14-day statutory minimum on a UK distance sale
  • Refunds are promised within 3 working days of the parcel reaching the warehouse, which is faster than most of the shops in this category commit to in writing
  • The shop is properly stocked and actively merchandised, with current collaborations including a Jayda Cheaves x Sumwon dress at $20.14 and Playboy, Baby Phat, Everlast and MG Teen lines alongside the main range
  • The terms of use name the selling entity for each region outright, which is more disclosure than many fashion sites manage at all
  • robots.txt blocks almost nothing, so the policy documents can actually be read and checked by anyone who wants to

Where it falls short

  • missguided.co.uk no longer opens a UK shop: both UK addresses return an HTTP 302 to a United States storefront, and no pound price appears anywhere on it
  • No UK or GB market exists on the site — no country selector, no /market-gb path, and no UK variant in the site's own category sitemap across roughly 300 entries checked
  • Two live shipping pages give different delivery prices on the same day, so the shop has no single answer to what postage costs; we have printed none rather than pick one
  • Both shipping pages carry delivery date windows that had already passed on 20 November 2026, and neither prints a year
  • Clause 5.6 of the terms says “Only damaged or defective items can be returned”, contradicting the 30-day change-of-mind window the returns page advertises
  • Return postage is at the customer's own cost, no collection service is offered, and the table of return fees is published only inside an image, so the amounts cannot be read from the page at all
  • The non-returnable list covers bodysuits, lingerie, underwear, swimwear, jewellery, accessories and all clearance and final sale items, and nothing on a reduced listing says whether it is a final sale item
  • The contract is governed by the laws of Singapore and sends disputes to single-arbitrator arbitration seated in Singapore, with group claims excluded — a term the CMA's own guidance notes is automatically unfair against a consumer below £5,000 under section 91 of the Arbitration Act 1996
  • The United Kingdom is not named anywhere in the terms, so a British buyer is left in the “all other markets” category by elimination, contracting with a Singapore company
  • The privacy policy returned no readable text at either address tried, so no data controller, company number or UK representative could be identified
  • The only active UK company bearing the name is registered as a “74990 - Non-trading company”, and the company that held the brand has been in liquidation since 2022, with its liquidator removed by court order on 12 August 2026
  • Whether the shop will deliver to a British address at all could not be established from any published page, and no shipping table naming the United Kingdom exists

Common questions

Can I still shop at Missguided in the UK?

Not at a UK address. On 20 November 2026 both www.missguided.co.uk and missguided.co.uk returned an HTTP 302 redirect to missguided.com/market-us, a United States storefront that prices in dollars and declares itself en-US. No country selector, no /market-gb path and no pound price could be found on any page read. Whether the US shop will accept a British delivery address at checkout is not stated anywhere it publishes, so ask customer services before ordering.

What do Missguided dresses cost now?

From $9.65 at the bottom of the range, reduced from $10.73, up to around $37.71 for a satin jacquard mini on the first pages of a listing carrying 5,785 items across 73 pages. A code, SAVE15, takes a further 15% off any four items. All prices are in US dollars; there is no sterling price list.

How long do I have to return something?

The returns page says “You have 30 days to decide if an item is right for you”, running from delivery, with returns made at your own cost and refunds processed within 3 working days of the parcel arriving. Note that clause 5.6 of the same site's terms of use says “Only damaged or defective items can be returned”, which contradicts it. Bodysuits, lingerie, underwear, swimwear, jewellery, accessories and clearance or final sale items cannot be returned at all.

How much is delivery?

We cannot tell you, because the shop gives two answers. On 20 November 2026 one live shipping page offered free standard delivery over $70.00 with $5.99 below that and $12.90 express, while a second live shipping page at a different address offered free over $56.99 with $4.99 below that and $9.99 express. Rather than pick one, we have printed neither. Check the figure shown in your own basket at checkout.

Who owns Missguided?

Frasers Group's own Annual Report for the 52 weeks ended 28 April 2024 records “the disposal of the Missguided intellectual property in October 2023” and describes Missguided as a “non-core brand”, without naming a buyer or a price. The shop's own terms name SHEIN Distribution Corporation as the selling entity in the United States and SHEIN Distribution Canada Limited in Canada. Who holds the UK trade mark today we could not confirm: the Intellectual Property Office's register would not open on three attempts, and we will not substitute a commercial database for it.

Is there any way to buy Missguided clothes second-hand?

Not through the brand. No pre-owned, resale, vintage or preloved page appears in either of the site's own content sitemaps, in its 43,894-character terms or on any brand landing page we read, and there is no buy-back scheme or named resale partner anywhere on it. Second-hand Missguided pieces circulate on general resale marketplaces, but none of that is a route the brand itself offers or stands behind.

What happened to the original Missguided company?

Administrators were appointed over the trading company, number 03235904, in a filing dated 7 June 2022. It was renamed MGL REALISATIONS (2022) LIMITED, moved from administration to dissolution on 2 June 2024, and was dissolved by final Gazette notice on 2 September 2024. The company that held the brand, number 13797040, is still in liquidation four years on, with its liquidator removed by court order on 12 August 2026. One active company still carries the name, MISSGUIDED RETAIL LIMITED, number 12298767, registered at Shirebrook as a “74990 - Non-trading company”.

Our verdict

As a shop, Missguided still works: $9.65 dresses, 5,785 of them, honest discount flags, a stacking 15% code and 30 days to change your mind. As a British shop, it has gone. The .co.uk address is a redirect to an American storefront with no pound prices and no UK market, the contract behind it is governed by Singapore law and names the United Kingdom nowhere, and the paperwork argues with itself in two places that matter — two live shipping pages with different postage prices, and a terms clause saying only faulty goods can come back under a returns page offering thirty days to change your mind. Buy from it the way you would buy from any overseas site: on a card that gives you a chargeback, expecting to pay return postage you cannot find a figure for, and having asked customer services in writing whether they will post to Britain before you spend anything.
