Type motors.co.uk/used-cars into a browser today and you do not arrive at Motors. You get an HTTP 302 redirect to cazoo.co.uk/cars. That is the whole story in one line: the Motors brand was retired on 27 May 2026, and the business now trades solely as Cazoo.

The domain has not been switched off. It is being kept alive purely as a redirect until late 2026, which means old bookmarks, old links and old search results still function — they simply land somewhere else. After that window closes, they will not.

None of this was a failure. The company doing the redirecting is the one that bought the Cazoo name out of administration in June 2024 for around £5m and then decided the acquired brand had more consumer recognition than its own. Motors.co.uk Limited still exists as the legal entity — it just trades under a different name.

27 May 2026: the date the name stopped being used

The switch was deliberate and complete. Every consumer-facing touchpoint moved to Cazoo on that date, and the reasoning was brand equity: Cazoo's spontaneous awareness among UK used-car buyers runs at around 60%, which the company's chief executive Barry Judge has claimed makes it the second best-known used car marketplace in Britain behind Auto Trader.

Motors, despite nearly two decades of trading, never got close to that. Buying a collapsed rival's name and then adopting it is an unusual move, but on the awareness numbers it is a rational one.

Practically, this means anything you read about Motors.co.uk written before mid-2026 describes a website that no longer serves customers. There is no separate Motors inventory, no separate Motors account and no separate Motors app.

From DMGT start-up to buying a failed unicorn's name

  • 2007 — founded by DMGT, the Daily Mail and General Trust.
  • 2021 — acquired by O3 Industries / Novum Capital, who remain the owners.
  • 2023 — rebranded from "Motors.co.uk" to the shorter "Motors".
  • June 2024 — buys the Cazoo brand out of administration for around £5m.
  • 28 August 2024 — announces a partnership putting its stock on Parkers.
  • April 2025 — relaunches Cazoo as a marketplace, app first, with over 250,000 listings.
  • 27 May 2026 — retires the Motors brand entirely.

The arc is worth understanding because it explains the current shape of the market. Cazoo's 300,000+ monthly listings and 6,000+ dealers are Motors' listings and Motors' dealers, wearing a name that used to belong to a company that spent £400m and folded.

The one asset that survived intact: the five-site network

Motors' genuine differentiator was never its own website traffic. It was distribution. One dealer invoice bought advertising across MOTORS, eBay, Gumtree, Cazoo and Parkers — five destinations, one bill, one stock feed.

The pitch to dealers rested on a specific behavioural finding: the average car buyer visits 4.2 websites during a search. Rather than fight for the whole journey, the network aimed to be present at several points on it.

That network is untouched by the rebrand. It now sits under the Cazoo banner with one fewer destination in the list, and it remains the most distinctive thing about the group. It is also why searching Cazoo, Gumtree and Parkers as three separate exercises is largely wasted effort — you are looking at one pool three times.

Dealer pricing for the network is not published. No public rate card exists, so any dealer weighing it against Auto Trader's £2,994 monthly sector average should request current pricing directly.

What to do with an old bookmark, saved search or dealer account

For buyers:

  • Old motors.co.uk links still resolve, but only until the redirect is retired in late 2026. Update anything you rely on now.
  • Saved searches and alerts should be recreated on Cazoo. Do not assume they migrated silently.
  • The cars you were watching are almost certainly still listed — same dealers, same stock, different domain.

For dealers:

  • Your advertising contract sits with Motors.co.uk Limited, which still exists and still trades — under the Cazoo name.
  • Placement across eBay, Gumtree and Parkers continues as before.
  • Check that any of your own marketing, forecourt signage or website badges still pointing at Motors are updated before the redirect window closes.
