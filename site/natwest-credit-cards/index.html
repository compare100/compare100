Types of NatWest Cards

NatWest provides distinct categories of credit cards, each tailored to different consumer needs:

Rewards Credit Cards: These cards allow you to earn points on your purchases. You can then redeem these points for various benefits. For instance, redemption options might include cashback, vouchers for retailers, or travel benefits. This type of card is ideal if you regularly spend and want to see tangible returns.


Low APR Credit Cards: Conversely, if you anticipate carrying a balance from month to month, a low APR (Annual Percentage Rate) card helps keep interest costs down. This can save you money over time compared to cards with higher interest rates. Consequently, these cards are a smart choice for managing ongoing credit use.


Balance Transfer Credit Cards: These cards are excellent tools for debt management. They allow you to move existing debt from other credit cards to a NatWest card. You can then benefit from a 0% interest promotional period on that transferred balance. Clearly, this opportunity helps you pay down debt faster without accruing additional interest charges.

Important Considerations Before Applying

While NatWest Credit Cards offer many advantages, it's crucial to consider these points:

Eligibility Criteria: Like all credit cards, approval depends on your credit score and financial situation. NatWest will assess your creditworthiness during the application process. Therefore, review the eligibility requirements carefully beforehand.
Promotional Period Expiry: For 0% interest cards (both balance transfer and purchase), remember that standard interest rates will apply after the promotional period ends. Consequently, plan to clear your balance before this time to avoid charges.
Fees and Charges: Although some cards have no annual fees, other charges might apply. For example, foreign transaction fees or cash advance fees could be relevant. Always review the terms and conditions thoroughly.
Impact on Credit Score: Applying for new credit cards can temporarily affect your credit score. As a result, only apply for cards you genuinely need and can manage responsibly.
