Key Features of P&O Cruises Worldwide Cruises

P&O Cruises Worldwide Cruises stands out due to its comprehensive packages and diverse options:

Vast Global Reach

Sail to 200+ destinations across 6 continents. For instance, popular routes include the Norwegian Fjords, Caribbean, Mediterranean, and even extensive World Cruises or Grand Voyages.

Affordable Starting Fares

Cruises are available from £299 per person for a 7-night trip. Indeed, this makes a sea adventure highly accessible and budget-friendly.

All-Inclusive Packages

Benefit from extensive all-inclusive packages. Typically, these include fine dining, a wide range of entertainment, and kids’ clubs.

Modern & Diverse Fleet

Explore the world aboard modern ships like Arvia and Iona (their newest Excel-class vessels). These ships feature innovative designs, including the SkyDome with a retractable roof. Furthermore, P&O also offers adults-only ships like Arcadia and Aurora for a more serene experience.

Gourmet Dining

Enjoy fine dining with diverse options, from casual eateries to specialty restaurants (some at extra cost). Specifically, Arvia and Iona boast over 30 places to eat and drink.

West End-Style Entertainment

Witness captivating West End-style shows, live music, and unique performances. Thus, every evening offers unique entertainment across the fleet.

Flexible Options

Options are available for both adults-only (on ships like Aurora and Arcadia) and family-friendly cruises (on ships like Arvia, Iona, Britannia, Azura, and Ventura). Therefore, whether you seek tranquility or adventure, this service caters to your needs.

Low Deposit & Flexible Cancellations

Book your cruise with a low deposit. Additionally, benefit from flexible cancellation policies, providing peace of mind.

The P&O Difference: Tailored Journeys & British Heritage

P&O Cruises Worldwide Cruises differentiates itself with specialized experiences and a unique appeal:

Adults-Only Cruises

For a truly relaxing escape, dedicated adults-only ships like Aurora and Arcadia offer sophisticated environments. Here, focus is on gourmet dining, spa experiences, and tranquil spaces.

Family-Friendly Adventures

Their family-friendly ships feature extensive kids' clubs (The Reef, with age-specific groups like Splashers, Surfers, Scubas, and Scene) and dedicated activities. For instance, Arvia and Iona boast splash zones, adventure parks, climbing walls, and even a multi-sensory submarine simulation (Mission Control on Arvia).

Iconic Ships & Onboard Innovations

Experience vessels like Arvia and Iona. These feature attractions such as the SkyDome (a retractable glass roof with a pool by day and entertainment by night), Altitude Skywalk (high ropes), and cinemas.

British Heritage

With over 180 years of history, P&O Cruises embodies a strong British cruising heritage. This ensures a familiar and welcoming onboard atmosphere for UK travelers.

Commitment to Sustainability

Newer ships like Arvia and Iona are powered by Liquified Natural Gas (LNG). Consequently, they significantly reduce environmental emissions.

Important Considerations Before Booking Your Cruise

While P&O Cruises Worldwide Cruises offers compelling benefits, it’s crucial to be aware of these points:

All-Inclusive Specifics

Carefully review what "all-inclusive" entails for your chosen package. Therefore, confirm which drinks, gratuities, and excursions are included in your specific fare type.

Ship Specific Features

Not all ships offer every amenity (e.g., Mission Control is only on Arvia, adults-only ships differ). Consequently, check the exact ship details for your chosen voyage.

Onboard Spending

While many amenities are included, specialty restaurants, spa treatments, and certain activities may incur extra charges. Hence, budget accordingly for these.

Cabin Selection

Understand the different cabin categories (interior, ocean view, balcony, suite) and their varying amenities/prices. Thus, choose what best suits your comfort level.
