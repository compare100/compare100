Santander Edge Account

In the dynamic landscape of UK banking, the Santander Edge Account emerges as a compelling option. It is specifically designed for those who want their current account to work harder. Launched to cater to savvy spenders and savers this year, this account uniquely blends valuable cashback rewards with a standout interest rate on savings. If you're looking for an account that puts money back in your pocket and helps your savings grow, the Santander Edge Account merits your attention. Therefore, at Compare100, we'll explore every facet of this offering. This helps you determine if it's the best current account for your financial strategy.

Key Features of the Santander Edge Account

The Santander Edge Account offers a robust set of benefits. These aim to provide tangible value:

  • Generous Cashback on Bills: You can earn 1% cashback on your essential household bills. This includes council tax, gas and electricity, water, and broadband payments. The cashback in this category we cap at £10 per month. This means you could get up to £10 back just on your regular outgoings.
  • Cashback on Everyday Spending:Additionally, the account rewards your daily spending. You'll receive 1% cashback on qualifying debit card purchases. This covers spending at supermarkets, fuel stations, and on travel. This category also has a monthly cap of £10.
    • Combined Potential: In total, when both categories are maximised, you could earn up to £20 cashback each month.
  • Attractive Edge Saver Interest: A significant draw is the linked Santander Edge Saver. This easy-access savings account offers a highly competitive 7% AER interest on balances up to £4,000. You can easily manage this saver directly within the Santander mobile app. Consequently, this makes it an excellent choice for keeping an emergency fund or short-term savings.
  • Monthly Account Fee: The Edge Account carries a £3 monthly fee. This fee applies regardless of how much you deposit or spend. However, the potential cashback and interest earnings are designed to significantly outweigh this fee for most active users.
  • Standard Banking Services: The account offers all expected fee-free banking services without additional charges. This covers direct debits, standing orders, and UK debit card transactions. Santander also provides a contactless debit card for convenient payments.
  • Robust Mobile Banking: Santander provides a highly-rated mobile app. It allows for seamless money management. You can check balances, make payments, and track your spending.

Who is This Account Best Suited For?

This account is an excellent choice for individuals who:

  • Pay Essential Bills: If your monthly household bills (council tax, utilities) total £1,000 or more, you can maximise the bills cashback.
  • Are Regular Spenders: Those who use their debit card frequently for groceries, fuel, and travel can benefit from the spending cashback.
  • Maintain Savings Balances: If you typically keep up to £4,000 in readily accessible savings, the 7% AER interest offers substantial returns compared to many other easy-access options.
  • Are Comfortable with a Monthly Fee: Customers who understand that the potential cashback and interest can offset the £3 fee will find value here.

Important Considerations for the Edge Account

While the Santander Edge Account offers impressive rewards, it's vital to understand its nuances:

  • The Monthly Fee: Santander does not waivethe £3 fee. Ensure your combined cashback and interest earnings are enough to make the account worthwhile for your specific financial activity.
  • Cashback Caps: Remember that Santander caps each cashback category (bills and spending) is capped at £10 per month. Even so, if you spend more than £1,000 in a category, you won't earn more cashback beyond the cap.
  • Savings Cap: The attractive 7% AER interest rate only applies to balances up to £4,000 in the Edge Saver. Funds above this amount will earn a standard, much lower rate.

How to Open a Santander Edge Account

Opening a Santander Edge Account is a straightforward process:

  1. Check Eligibility: Applicants must be UK residents aged 18 or over. Santander will also conduct identity and credit checks.
  2. Apply Online or In-Branch: You can apply for the account directly through the official Santander website or by visiting a Santander branch.
  3. Provide Details: You will need personal details, address history, and information about your income and existing banking.
  4. Fund Your Account: Once approved, you can start using your account. Consider setting up direct debits for bills and salary payments to maximize your cashback potential.

Comparing the Santander Edge Account

The Santander Edge Account offers a unique combination of cashback and savings interest. However, it's crucial to compare it against other UK current accounts to find your perfect match. For example, consider:

  • High-Interest Accounts: Compare its 7% AER on £4,000 with other savings accounts that might offer higher rates on larger balances, though perhaps with more restrictions.
  • Cashback Alternatives: Other banks or credit cards also offer cashback. Weigh up which scheme best suits your spending habits.
  • Perks-Based Accounts: Accounts like the Club Lloyds Account offer lifestyle benefits instead of direct cashback.
  • Fee-Free Digital Banking: If you prefer no fees at all, even without the associated rewards, a digital option like the Monzo Current Account might be more suitable.
