Important Considerations Before Applying

While Santander's current mortgage range presents compelling benefits, it's crucial to be aware of these points:

Representative APR:

The 3.99% rate is representative and subject to LTV. Your actual rate will depend on your specific circumstances and the product you choose. Therefore, ensure you check your personalized offer.

Product Fees:

Some Santander mortgage deals carry a fee (e.g., £999, £1,999). Consequently, factor this into your overall cost.

Fixed-Term Commitment:

Fixed-rate mortgages lock your rate for a set period. Early repayment charges might apply if you repay your mortgage in full or overpay beyond a certain limit during this term.

Rates Vary by LTV:

The lowest rates typically require a higher deposit (lower LTV). Rates will increase for higher LTV products. Hence, assess your available deposit.

Eligibility Criteria:

Like all mortgage products, approval depends on your financial history, income (minimum £25k for BTL), and credit checks. Santander will conduct a thorough assessment.
