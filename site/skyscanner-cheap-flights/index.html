Important Considerations Before Using Skyscanner

While Skyscanner Cheap Flights offers incredible convenience and potential savings, it's vital to consider these points:

Skyscanner finds the deals; it does not sell tickets directly. Therefore, you complete your booking with airlines or third-party travel sites. Always confirm the final price and terms on the booking site.

Fare Fluctuations:

Flight prices can change rapidly. Consequently, if you find a great deal, it's often wise to book quickly before it disappears.

Partner Redirection:

You will be redirected off Skyscanner to complete your purchase. Hence, be aware of different booking site policies and customer service options.

Consolidator vs. Airline:

Sometimes the lowest prices are from online travel agencies (OTAs) rather than airlines directly. Thus, weigh the pros and cons of booking with an OTA vs. the airline itself.
